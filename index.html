Color Escape: World Tour – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026

In short. Color Escape: World Tour has no accounts, no advertising, no in-app purchases, no analytics, no crash reporting and no chat. The app does not use the internet to play and does not send any information about you or your game to us or to anyone else. Your progress and sound settings are saved only on your own device. We do not collect personal information, including from children.

1. Who we are

Color Escape: World Tour (the “app”) is a puzzle game published by Моника Дакова (Monika Dakova), trading as Serdica Studio (“we”, “us”). Contact: contact@serdicastudio.com.

2. What we collect

Nothing. The app does not ask for or receive your name, email address, age, location, photos, contacts, advertising identifier or any other personal information. There is no sign-in, and we operate no server for the app, so we hold no data about you.

3. What is stored on your device

So that you can pick up where you left off, the app saves the following in its private storage on your device (Android shared preferences or the iOS equivalent):

  • Game progress: the highest level you have unlocked, your best number of moves and star rating for each completed level, and which city-unlock celebrations you have already seen. If a saved file cannot be read, the app may keep a recovery copy of it on the device.
  • Sound settings: whether music and sound effects are on and their volume.

This information stays on your device and is not sent to us. It does not identify you.

4. Music and sound

All music and sound effects are bundled inside the app and play from your device. The app asks your device’s audio system how to share sound with other apps (for example, so that your own music or a call is handled sensibly), but it does not record audio and does not use the microphone.

5. Network activity and third parties

The app contains no advertising, analytics, attribution, crash-reporting or social-media code, and no third-party service receives data from the app. The Android release of the app does not request the internet permission. The only time the app involves the internet is if you tap “Privacy Policy” in Settings: your device then opens this page in your web browser, and that visit is handled by your browser and by the page’s host (GitHub Pages), which may log standard connection details such as your IP address under its own privacy policy. We do not receive that information.

Your device or operating system may also communicate with its own services, for example to make backups, according to your device settings. We do not receive any of this data.

6. Permissions

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files, location or accounts. On Android it uses only the system’s ability to check whether a network connection exists, which is added automatically by the app framework and is not used to send data.

7. Advertising and purchases

The current version has no advertising and no in-app purchases. If either is added in the future, we will update this policy before that version is released.

8. Deleting your data

  • In Settings, “Reset Progress” erases your levels and stars on the device.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the data it stored. However, Android or iOS backups (for example Google backup or iCloud), if enabled on your device or account, may keep a copy that is restored when you reinstall. You can manage or delete such backups in your device or account settings.
  • Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing on our side to delete.

9. Children

The game is suitable for players of all ages, including children. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics, purchases, chat or any way to contact other players, and it does not collect personal information from anyone. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact contact@serdicastudio.com and we will delete it.

10. Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold personal data, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, export or erase. If you are in a region with data-protection rights (such as the EU, UK or California) and have a question, you can contact us at the address below.

11. Changes

If the app’s behaviour changes (for example, if ads, purchases, analytics or accounts are added), we will update this policy and its effective date before the change is released.

12. Contact
