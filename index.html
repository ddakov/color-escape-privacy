Color Escape: World Tour – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026

In short. Color Escape: World Tour has no accounts, no advertising, no subscriptions, no analytics, no crash reporting and no chat. Levels 1–50 are free; the remaining levels are unlocked by one optional one-time purchase, which is handled entirely by Google Play (or, on iOS, the App Store) and not by us. We do not collect personal information, and we do not receive your game progress, settings or payment details. Your progress and settings are saved only on your own device. When you buy or restore the purchase, Google Play (or Apple) processes information about you and your purchase under its own privacy policy.

1. Who we are

Color Escape: World Tour (the “app”) is a puzzle game published by Моника Дакова (Monika Dakova), trading as Serdica Studio (“we”, “us”). Contact: contact@serdicastudio.com.

2. What we collect

Nothing. The app does not ask for or receive your name, email address, age, location, photos, contacts, advertising identifier or any other personal information. There is no sign-in, and we operate no server for the app, so we hold no data about you.

3. What is stored on your device

So that you can pick up where you left off, the app saves the following in its private storage on your device (Android shared preferences or the iOS equivalent):

  • Game progress: the highest level you have unlocked, your best number of moves and star rating for each completed level, and which celebrations and first-time tips you have already seen. If a saved file cannot be read, the app may keep a recovery copy of it on the device.
  • Sound and vibration settings: whether music, sound effects and vibration are on, and their volume.
  • Purchase record: if you buy or restore the Complete World Tour, a small record that you own it (the product name and the purchase identifier supplied by the store). It lets the game unlock the levels, including offline, and is re-checked with the store when the app starts.

This information stays on your device and is not sent to us. It does not identify you.

4. Music and sound

All music and sound effects are bundled inside the app and play from your device. The app asks your device’s audio system how to share sound with other apps (for example, so that your own music or a call is handled sensibly), but it does not record audio and does not use the microphone. If vibration is on, the app asks your phone for short vibrations at moments such as completing a level; this uses no personal information.

5. Network activity and third parties

The app contains no advertising, analytics, attribution, crash-reporting or social-media code, and the app sends no data to us or to any server operated by us. The app does use the internet in two ways:

  • Google Play Billing (Android) / StoreKit (iOS): to offer, buy and restore the Complete World Tour, the app uses the Google Play Billing library (on iOS, Apple’s StoreKit) to talk to Google Play (or Apple). The app asks the store for the product’s price and for your purchases of this app, and the store tells the app whether you own the product. In doing this, Google (or Apple) receives and processes information such as your store account, device and purchase details under its own privacy policy (Google, Apple). We do not receive your payment details, name or email address.
  • This policy: if you tap “Privacy Policy” in Settings, your device opens this page in your web browser, and that visit is handled by your browser and by the page’s host (GitHub Pages), which may log standard connection details such as your IP address under its own privacy policy. We do not receive that information.

Your device or operating system may also communicate with its own services, for example to make backups, according to your device settings. We do not receive any of this data.

6. Permissions

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files, location or accounts. On Android the app declares the internet and network-state permissions and the Google Play Billing permission (com.android.vending.BILLING), which are used only for the store communication and the policy link described above.

7. Advertising and purchases

The app has no advertising, no subscriptions, no coins and no paid hints (Smart Hints are free). Levels 1–50 (New York and Paris) are free. The remaining levels (51–250) are unlocked by one optional, one-time in-app purchase called “Complete World Tour”. The payment is processed entirely by Google Play (or, on iOS, the App Store) under their own terms and privacy policies. We never see or store your card or other payment details. “Restore Purchases” asks the store for purchases made with your store account so that you can recover the unlock on a new or reinstalled device. Resetting your progress does not remove the purchase. If advertising or other kinds of purchases are added in the future, we will update this policy before that version is released.

8. Deleting your data

  • In Settings, “Reset Progress” erases your levels and stars on the device.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the data it stored. However, Android or iOS backups (for example Google backup or iCloud), if enabled on your device or account, may keep a copy that is restored when you reinstall. You can manage or delete such backups in your device or account settings.
  • Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing on our side to delete.

9. Children

The game is suitable for players of all ages, including children. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics, chat or any way to contact other players, and we do not collect personal information from anyone. The only purchase is the optional one-time Complete World Tour, processed by Google Play (or Apple), which may process data about a store account under its own policies; parents and guardians can control purchases with the store’s own tools, such as Google Play parental controls and purchase authentication, or Apple’s Screen Time and “Ask to Buy”. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact contact@serdicastudio.com and we will delete it.

10. Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold personal data, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, export or erase. If you are in a region with data-protection rights (such as the EU, UK or California) and have a question, you can contact us at the address below.

11. Changes

If the app’s behaviour changes (for example, if ads, analytics, subscriptions or accounts are added), we will update this policy and its effective date before the change is released.
